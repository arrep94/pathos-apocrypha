# Art credits for added content

## Spell and ability icons

The glyph art added by this fork for new spells is derived from **game-icons.net**, licensed under
[Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported (CC BY 3.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).
The icons were recoloured to the school colour and rendered at the size Pathos uses; no other change
was made to the artwork.

## Spellbook cover art

Covers for new book appearances are recolourings of the existing Pathos book tiles of the same
tileset, generated by `pathos-dev/tools/bookart.py`. They inherit the licence of the original
Pathos artwork (CC BY-NC 4.0, see `license.txt`).
